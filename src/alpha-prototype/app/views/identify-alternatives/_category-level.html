A device type holds either branded device systems (made of components, each with variants) or branded devices (single devices, with variants). Documents attach at the branded level. These pages list device types; the device type page lists the branded ones. ── Layout ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Two layouts, one per mode. Listing device types (the usual case), aligned with RS Components and eBay: - The h1 gives the count and the category: "212 device types in Bone/Joint fixation implants". It sits over the results it describes. The results column comes first in the source, floated right, so the h1 is still the first heading screen readers meet. - A one-third sidebar, as on current/search-results. First, category navigation styled after the NHS digital service manual's sidebar: this category's subcategories, or at the end of a branch, this category among its siblings. It replaces the separate Subcategories block. Then the filters, below a section break. Components/checkboxes.md names "a page of search results where users need to see and change search filters" as a place for small checkboxes. - Results on the right, two-thirds wide. The list holds every GMDN term beneath this level, not only those filed directly at it, so someone can find a device type without knowing the right subcategory. The tree and the filters are two ways to narrow it. Subcategories only (hideDevices, for broad levels where people are still narrowing down): heading, then the subcategories in two columns, as before. Over 6, a page can chunk them under headings (Content/formatting.md: lists of no more than 6 items where possible). Group names are ours, not GMDN's, and need a clinical check. Subcategories are A to Z, since GMDN's order is not explained anywhere, so people cannot predict where an item will be. By analogy with checkboxes.md and radios.md: "If in doubt, order alphabetically". Results are a 2-column table, device and number of suppliers, per Components/table.md. Trust and evidence data belong at the branded-device level, where they are connected. Page size is 25, matching current/ (pageSize in current/routes.js). Components/pagination.md leaves the page size to the service, asks for the page number in the , and says not to show pagination for 1 page. The sort control needs its own button. Submitting on change would move people without warning (WCAG 3.2.2). ── Filters ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── One group per GMDN root node, from the "Also in categories" lists in Dylan's samples. Group names and options are GMDN wording, reproduced exactly, first level only so each group stays short enough to scan. Ordered by how much each group is likely to narrow the list: clinical area and invasiveness first, then attributes. Power comes last, since almost every term in Tissue reparation devices is Non-active. Left out on purpose: - Device Function is the tree these pages sit in, shown as subcategories. - Names Index groups terms by device family (Bandages, Binders, Sutures), which repeats the subcategories in other words. - GMDN has no "non-invasive" node in the samples, so there is no option for terms outside the Invasiveness/Implantability options. Not invented. Each level lists only the options that have results, and leaves out a group when every device type beneath shares the same option, since it cannot narrow anything. Deeper levels have fewer groups as a result.
