{# Branded device page sketch. Started on 2026-10-19 as a copy of add-contact-details/branded-device.html, which is left as it is, then restructured around how people use the page. Primary scenario: designing and running a trial. Writing a business case is secondary, usually a return visit. Compass is not a marketplace. The page feeds an acquisition person's judgement, and a good visit ends with a lead, or enough confidence to shortlist the device. A summary and navigation hub, not every piece of data in a long list, so it stays the same size whether a device has no trust experience or hundreds of trusts. Each lead section answers only "is it worth clicking?", so it surfaces the number of trusts, the key signal of how widely adopted the device is (Dylan), in the card title. People, contracts and documents are supplementary: how much useful information is behind it, as key and value under the title. Documents are 1 "Shared documents" row, listed by type (evaluations, business cases, clinical trials or pilot studies, other), because the type decides what a document is good for, e.g. an evaluation for designing a trial, and "shared documents" says they are files people can get at. Only types that exist are listed. Summary cards (Components/summary-list.md, "Summary cards"): a card with a title and a summary list, then 1 link through to the trusts page whose heading it mirrors. Follows Dylan's data model: leads are framed by trust, and within a trust by people, who are sometimes linked to contracts and sometimes to documents. Both lead sections have that same shape. The page is centred on trusts, so each section has 1 way in, a link to its trusts. If the business case visit needs a by-type view (e.g. all business cases), it is a filter on the trusts page. Order, from Dylan's data hierarchy: 1. What it is. Hard data, organised by us. Orientation comes first. 2. Trusts with experience of this device (exact matches). No inferred status like "Bought" or "Used": a contract proves buying, not use. "People", not "contacts" (Dylan's term). Within a trust, things hang off people: a person is sometimes linked to contracts, sometimes to documents. People include procurement leads from contract records, marked as such on the trusts page. 3. Trusts with contracts in the same or related categories (Dylan's wording: "bought related devices" is not necessarily true). Contracts matched only by supplier and contract category, because contract categories are not GMDN. Separate so weaker leads are not mistaken for direct experience. Document pages carry the download. The summary at the top has only device type, supplier and acquisition cost (Dylan's call). Everything else about the device (supplied as, variants, certifications and compliance, specifications, further costs) is on the specifications and costs page. The supplier name links to the supplier page. Every figure states its source. Each card ends in a short "View trusts" link (Dylan's approved design), with no visually hidden text (Dylan: universal design); the card title above it says which trusts. "Add your trust's experience" is a smaller secondary button inside the trusts card, so it does not outweigh the "View trusts" link, the main action there (Components/buttons.md allows smaller buttons in a card). nhsuk-frontend 10.1 has no small button, so it uses the repo's app-button--small, as identify-alternatives/mas-plif.html does. "Contact us" is on this page only (Dylan), with the wording copied as it is from current/product-page.html. The current page puts it in a non-urgent care card; care cards are for helping people decide when and where to get care (Components/card.md), so here it is plain content. The email is a mailto link, since contacting the team is the action. The tabs from the copy are gone. Components/tabs.md says to test content without tabs first, and not to use them when the 1st tab is not the most relevant. Following the nomenclature, the model (InsuFlow Pro) is the brand and the brand trade name is a variant. The acquisition cost is copied from the current product page. The breadcrumb skips the device function levels between "Device function" and the device type, which we do not have for this term. Static mockup. Pages one level down go to "#". The trust, people, contract and document counts are invented (the database has 1 business case and 1 contact for this device).
